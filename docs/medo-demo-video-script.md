## Title

Hackathon Submission Checklist Builder — MeDo demo

## Opening, 0:00-0:20

Hi, this is Hackathon Submission Checklist Builder, a MeDo-built Work & Productivity app for solo hackathon builders.

The problem is simple: builders often discover eligibility, payout, demo, or final-submit blockers too late. This app turns a hackathon page and builder profile into a structured readiness checklist before the deadline.

## Intake, 0:20-0:55

Here is the intake form. A builder enters the hackathon name, URL, organizer, deadline, residence, nationality or citizenship, prize type, available payout rails, required deliverables, rules notes, and build notes.

For this demo, I seeded the Build with MeDo Hackathon. The sample includes the May 20, 2026 deadline, the Work & Productivity category, cash prize pool, and required deliverables such as a working MeDo app and public project URL.

## Risk checklist, 0:55-1:35

Next is the Risk Checklist. The app groups checks into eligibility, payout, and human approval boundaries.

Eligibility checks include residence, nationality, sanctions restrictions, age of majority, and whether solo participation is allowed.

Payout checks include whether the prize method is clear, whether it matches the builder's available rails, whether there is PayPal-only or specific-bank risk, whether KYC or tax forms are required, and whether hardware shipping or customs could be an issue.

The important detail is that the app does not pretend risky actions are complete. In this sample, payout compatibility still needs review, and Devpost Join, rules acceptance, and final submission remain human approval steps.

## Submission assets, 1:35-2:10

The Submission Asset Generator creates draft text that the builder can copy into Devpost: a short description, long description, problem statement, how MeDo was used, demo flow, public URL checklist, repo checklist, and organizer questions.

This reduces last-minute writing work while still keeping the builder in control of final submission.

## Final dashboard, 2:10-2:45

Finally, the dashboard summarizes whether the project is Ready, Needs Review, or Blocked.

For the Build with MeDo sample, the status is Needs Review because payout compatibility and final Devpost terms acceptance still need human confirmation. That is intentional. The app helps builders move fast without crossing legal, account, wallet, KYC, or final-submit boundaries automatically.

## Closing, 2:45-3:00

Hackathon Submission Checklist Builder shows how MeDo can quickly create a practical workflow app from natural-language prompts. It helps builders choose better opportunities, prepare cleaner submissions, and avoid preventable disqualification.

Thank you.
